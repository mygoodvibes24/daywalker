# DAYWALKER: BLOOD AND MOON
### Story Bible & Game Design Concept — v0.1

> *"The sun was supposed to kill me. It just made me angry."*

---

## 1. The Pitch

A **cinematic, ultra-realistic open-world action-horror game** with story choices that change who lives and who dies.

You play **Elijah "Sol" Marrow** — a fast, lean, trenchcoat-wearing hybrid born in the moment his mother was turned. He is half human, half vampire: he can walk in the sun, heal by biting, and move faster than anything in the city. From his hidden lair under the city, he hunts in a war between two monsters that have kept the peace for 200 years, until someone starts murdering both sides and blaming him.

**Tone:** dark, stylish, grounded. Camera work, faces and lighting like a prestige horror film; combat built on speed.

**Look and feel targets:** photoreal faces and performance capture, choices that ripple forward and characters who can die for good, and fast, smooth movement and combat. Speed is the core fantasy.

---

## 2. The Hero

### Elijah "Sol" Marrow — The Daywalker
- **Look:** Tall, slim and wiry, a lean fighter's build. Shoulder-length locs, often tied back for a fight. Long black leather trenchcoat, black fitted clothes, dark glasses for the light sensitivity he still has. Calm, still face; when he moves, it's sudden.
- **Age:** Looks 30. Is 47.
- **Personality:** Quiet, dry humor, controlled. He talks less than everyone else in the room and still owns it. Underneath, he's terrified of becoming the thing he hunts.
- **Origin:** His mother, **Grace Marrow**, a trauma nurse, was bitten while pregnant. She died giving birth. Sol was raised in foster care, didn't know what he was until his teens, and fed on someone for the first time at 17. He's been running from that night ever since.
- **The Hunger:** He needs blood. He can live on bagged blood at the lair, but in the field, **biting is how he heals.** The more he bites, the stronger he gets and the closer he gets to losing himself.

### Movement (He is NOT slow)
| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Blood Dash** | Short, near-instant burst. Chain up to 3. Leaves a smear of motion blur and coat-flare. |
| **Wall Run / Vault** | Free parkour across rooftops, fire escapes, train cars. |
| **Slide-Strike** | Slide under an attack into a takedown. |
| **Bullet-Time Sense** | Sees the world slow for a moment when danger spikes (a perfect dodge triggers it). |
| **Sun Sprint** | In daylight, stamina regenerates faster. Daytime is *his* time. |
| **Grapple Blade** | A chain blade for swinging, pulling enemies, and zipping to ledges. |

## 3. The Lair — **The Undercroft**
Below the old **St. Jerome Cathedral**, past sealed 1890s subway tunnels, is a flooded Gothic crypt Sol has turned into his base. This is his Batcave.

- **The Altar Room:** the planning table, a wall of city maps, evidence strings, and the faction-influence map.
- **The Armory:** silver-edged blades, UV grenades, wolfsbane rounds, coats you can upgrade.
- **The Blood Bank:** cold storage. How you stock it, by buying, stealing or biting, affects the story.
- **The Lab:** Dr. Nia Okafor's domain (see allies). Crafting serums and upgrades.
- **The Training Hall:** combat challenges and movement trials.
- **The Garage:** a matte-black 1970 muscle car and a stripped-down motorcycle for fast travel.
- **The Crypt Wall:** names of everyone Sol has killed. You watch it grow, and the game keeps count.

The lair **changes visually** as the story goes on: more allies move in, damage from a raid, candles for the dead.

---

## 4. The City — **Port Vesper**
A fictional rain-soaked Southern port city: old money, jazz bars, bayou edges, and a rotting industrial waterfront.

| District | Controlled By | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **The Velvet Mile** | Vampires | Neon nightclubs, penthouses, private galleries. Beautiful and deadly. |
| **Old Quarter** | Contested | Cobblestone streets, cathedral, voodoo shops, the Undercroft entrance. |
| **Hollow Pines** | Werewolves | Forest edge, trailer parks, logging yards, family homes. |
| **The Docks** | Smugglers / Both | Shipping containers, blood-trafficking ring, fight pits. |
| **The Bayou** | Nobody | Swamp ruins, an old plantation house, the oldest secrets. |
| **Midtown** | Humans (mostly) | Police HQ, hospitals, the place where the war spills over. |

**Day/Night Cycle matters:**
- **Day:** Vampires sleep. Sol raids nests, tracks leads, talks to humans. Werewolves look human, and anyone could be one.
- **Night:** Vampires rule the Velvet Mile. Hunting parties roam.
- **Full Moon (every 7 in-game days):** werewolves are forced to change. The city goes into lockdown chaos and the biggest set pieces happen.

## 5. The Enemies

### 🩸 THE VELVET COURT (Vampires)
Old, rich, charming and theatrical. They hide in plain sight as the city's elite.

**Count Lucien Duvall — The Showman King**
- 300-year-old vampire lord who came over from the Caribbean on a ghost ship in the 1720s.
- **Personality:** Hilarious, smooth, magnetic, vain. He tells jokes while he kills you. He loves disguises: he can **shapeshift his face** into anyone (a preacher, a cop, an old woman) and enjoys fooling people far too much.
- He wears tailored suits, has long hair and a perfect smile. He seduces before he slaughters.
- **What he wants:** a *bride* to rule beside him, and a cure for the sun. Sol's blood is that cure.
- **Signature line:** *"Brother, I'm not evil. I'm just… well-fed."*

**Countess Seraphine Duvall — The Real Threat**
- Lucien's ex-queen, presumed dead for a century. She's back.
- Cold, brilliant and patient. She is the hidden villain behind the murders.

**Court Lieutenants (Bosses):**
- **Marcus "Velvet" Crane:** nightclub owner, blood dealer, and a speed vampire who is Sol's mirror.
- **The Twins (Lyra & Vex):** a sister duo of assassins who share one mind.
- **Father Abernathy:** a vampire priest running a cult of human worshippers in the Old Quarter.

**Vampire enemy types:** Thralls (feral, fast, weak), Courtiers (elegant duelists), Gargoyles (bat-form flyers), Blood Knights (armored elite), and Mimics (they look like civilians until they don't).

### 🐺 THE HOLLOW PACK (Werewolves)
These are working families: mechanics, nurses, teenagers, your neighbor. The curse runs in bloodlines. They look completely normal, and **you can't always tell who's a wolf until they turn.**

**Mother Wren Hollow — The Alpha**
- A fierce, gray-haired matriarch who runs the Pack like a family business. She believes vampires slaughtered her husband, and that Sol did it.

**Dax Hollow — The Heir**
- Wren's son. He's hot-headed, wants war, and has the most brutal transformation in the game.

**Werewolf enemy types:** Turned (half-form, fast, savage), Full Wolves (huge, tanky), Alphas (bosses who summon the pack), and Skinwalkers (rogue wolves who turn mid-conversation and look like humans until it's too late).

**Transformations** are full, real-time, body-horror cinematics: bones cracking, skin splitting, eyes changing. Each named wolf has a unique change.

## 6. The Women of the Story

**Dr. Nia Okafor: The Ally and Brain** *(Alfred plus Oracle)*
- Hematologist who lost her medical license for researching "impossible blood." She found Sol bleeding out in an alley and patched him up instead of running.
- She runs the lab, makes serums, and talks to him over comms. She's warm and blunt and the only person who calls him "Elijah."
- **Choice-driven:** she's a possible romance, and she can be killed in Act 2.

**Rook Hollow: The Wolf**
- Wren's adopted daughter. She's a street racer and mechanic with a sharp tongue, and she hates being a wolf.
- She starts as an enemy who tries to kill Sol, and over the course of the game becomes a rival and maybe more.
- **Choice-driven:** she can become a full ally, a romance, or the next Alpha who leads the Pack against you.

**Detective Simone Reyes: The Human Law**
- Port Vesper homicide. She is tracking a "serial killer" who leaves bite marks: Sol.
- She doesn't believe in monsters, until she does.
- **Choice-driven:** expose the world to her or keep her in the dark. Either way it changes the ending.

**Countess Seraphine Duvall: The Villain** *(see above)*
- An elegant, terrifying femme fatale. She tries to seduce Sol to her side, and she's genuinely persuasive.

**Mama Odette: The Oracle**
- A bayou root-woman who knew Sol's mother. She knows the prophecy and speaks in riddles, and she's funnier than she lets on.

## 7. Core Mechanic — **THE BITE**

Biting is the heart of the game.

**How it works:**
- Stagger an enemy, then hold **[Bite]** for a brutal, cinematic feeding takedown that **restores health.**
- Bite mid-combat to heal instantly, but you're vulnerable for a second.
- Different blood gives different effects:

| Blood Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Human** | Big heal, raises the Hunger meter sharply. |
| **Vampire** | Medium heal, temporary speed boost. |
| **Werewolf** | Small heal, temporary rage/strength boost, but it burns (damage over time). |
| **Bagged (Lair)** | Safe heal item, limited supply. |

### The HUNGER Meter (Morality System)
- **Low Hunger: "Daywalker."** Sol stays human. Allies trust him and the city calls him a guardian. He gets better daylight abilities.
- **High Hunger: "Nightborn."** Sol grows more vampire. He gets stronger blood powers (mist dash, mind control, bat swarm), but allies fear him, some leave, and the vampires try to recruit him.
- **Biting innocents** spikes Hunger, and the game remembers who you fed on.
- If Hunger maxes out in combat, Sol goes into a **Frenzy**: unstoppable, but you lose control and might bite someone you love.

## 8. THE STORYLINE

### PROLOGUE: "Born in the Dark"
**1979, Port Vesper General Hospital.** A night shift. Nurse Grace Marrow, eight months pregnant, is attacked by a smiling stranger with a beautiful voice. She delivers her son as she dies. The doctors swear the baby's eyes flashed red.

**Present day.** Cold open: Sol storms a vampire blood-den under a nightclub. *(Tutorial: movement, combat, your first bite.)* He finds a werewolf chained inside, drained for sport. He frees her. It's **Rook**, and she bolts.

### ACT I: "The Truce Breaks"
- Port Vesper has a secret 200-year **Accord**: vampires take the night and the city, wolves take the woods and the full moon, and nobody touches humans openly.
- Someone murders **Wren Hollow's husband** and leaves a *vampire bite.* The next night, a **Velvet Court duke** is found torn apart by *claws.*
- Both crime scenes have one thing in common: **Sol's blood.** Someone is framing him.
- Now both factions are hunting the Daywalker, and Detective Reyes is too.
- Sol builds up the **Undercroft**, recruits **Nia**, and gets his first audience with **Count Lucien**, who is far too charming and offers him a seat at his table.
- **Act I climax: Full Moon Night #1.** The Hollow Pack attacks the Velvet Mile. Sol is caught in the middle of an open street war. **CHOICE:** save Lucien's people or save the werewolf families caught in the crossfire.

### ACT II: "Blood Ties"
- Mama Odette reveals the **prophecy:** *"A child of sun and blood will end the night or make it eternal."*
- Sol learns **Lucien was the vampire who bit his mother.** Lucien is his "father" by blood.
- Lucien knows it. He's been watching Sol his whole life and wants him as his heir. His blood could let every vampire walk in the sun.
- Rook and Sol are forced to team up to find the real killer. *(Co-op style missions, romance path opens.)*
- **The Raid:** someone leaks the Undercroft's location. Vampires and wolves hit it at once. The lair is half-destroyed. **CHOICE: save Nia or save Rook.** One of them may not make it, depending on your earlier choices.
- **Twist:** the murders were done by **Countess Seraphine**, Lucien's "dead" ex-queen. She's been building an army of **hybrids**, vampire-werewolf abominations made from stolen Pack bloodlines and Sol's own blood (taken from the crime scenes and the hospital in 1979).
- **Act II climax:** Sol confronts Lucien. Lucien didn't know about Seraphine and is genuinely scared for the first time. **CHOICE:** kill Lucien, spare him, or *drink from him* (a big Hunger spike and a power boost).

### ACT III: "Daybreak"
- Seraphine unleashes her hybrids on Midtown during an **eclipse**, the one time vampires can walk in daylight without Sol's blood. The city falls into chaos and the humans finally see everything.
- Sol must unite enemies against a bigger enemy:
  - **Convince Mother Wren** (or Rook, if she's Alpha now) to bring the Pack.
  - **Convince Lucien** (if alive) to turn on his queen.
  - **Convince Detective Reyes** to evacuate the city.
- **Final Mission: "The Eclipse":** an assault on the old bayou plantation, Seraphine's nest. A massive multi-faction battle, then a duel on a burning rooftop as the eclipse ends.
- **Final Boss:** Seraphine in three phases: her elegant human form, a blood-storm form, then a hybrid monster form.

### ENDINGS (based on Hunger, choices, and who's alive)
1. **"The Daywalker"** *(Low Hunger, allies alive):* Sol destroys the hybrid bloodline and refuses Lucien's throne. A new Accord is signed, with Sol as its guardian. The final shot is him walking out of the Undercroft into the sunrise, with Nia and/or Rook beside him.
2. **"The Sun King"** *(High Hunger):* Sol drinks Seraphine dry and takes the Velvet Court. He becomes the first vampire king who walks in daylight. Port Vesper belongs to him. The final shot is a throne, red eyes, and dark glasses coming off.
3. **"The Last Wolf Moon"** *(Rook romance, Pack alliance):* Sol and Rook lead the Pack and wipe out the vampires. Peace comes through genocide, and he isn't sure he was right.
4. **"Ashes"** *(Frenzy ending / everyone dead):* Sol loses control in the final fight. He wins, alone, in a dead city. The Crypt Wall is full.
5. **Secret ending: "My Father's Son":** spare Lucien in every encounter, and in the end Lucien sacrifices himself in the sun to save Sol. *"Tell 'em I went out looking good."*

**Post-credits hook:** Mama Odette lights a candle. *"There were two babies born that night, child."*

## 9. Butterfly Choices (Until-Dawn-style)
Every major choice is tracked in a **"Bloodline" journal** in the Undercroft, showing branching paths you've unlocked or lost.

Key choice points:
- Free Rook or leave her (Prologue)
- Who you save on Full Moon Night #1
- Bite the detective's partner or let him die
- Tell Nia the truth about your Hunger
- Save Nia or Rook in the Raid
- Kill, spare, or drink from Lucien
- Reveal monsters to the world or cover it up

**Any named character can die permanently**, including love interests.

## 10. Combat Summary
- **Fast, fluid melee:** blade combos, dodge-dash, counters, finishers. Fast and acrobatic, never heavy.
- **Tools:** silver blades (vs vampires), wolfsbane rounds (vs wolves), UV flash grenades, chain blade.
- **Bite takedowns** for healing.
- **Blood Powers** (unlocked by Hunger): Mist Dash, Blood Whip, Thrall Command, Bat Swarm.
- **Day Powers** (unlocked by staying human): Sunburst (a UV aura), Solar Blade, Daylight Regen.
- **Enemy weaknesses differ:** vampires are fast and fragile, wolves are tanky and relentless, hybrids are both.

## 11. Side Content
- **Nest Raids:** clear vampire dens in daylight.
- **Moon Hunts:** track rogue wolves before they kill on full moon nights.
- **Velvet Mile Nightclub Infiltration:** social stealth disguised among vampires (Lucien's favorite missions).
- **Street Races with Rook.**
- **Mama Odette's Hoodoo Quests:** lore and secret powers.
- **Case Files with Detective Reyes:** investigation missions.
- **Blood Trafficking Ring:** a long questline to shut down the Docks.

## 12. How to Actually Build It (Ultra-Realistic)

To reach photoreal, *Until Dawn*-level visuals:

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| Engine | **Unreal Engine 5** (Lumen lighting, Nanite geometry) |
| Realistic humans | **MetaHuman Creator** (free with UE5): build Sol, Nia, Rook, Lucien |
| Facial performance | **MetaHuman Animator**, which captures facial performance with an iPhone |
| Body motion | Rokoko / Move.ai suits, or mocap libraries |
| Werewolf transforms | Houdini plus Alembic simulations, or blendshape-driven morph rigs |
| Open world | UE5 World Partition, PCG framework for the city and bayou |
| Choices system | UE5 Blueprints or a dialogue plugin (e.g., Articy:draft, Yarn Spinner) |
| Combat | Gameplay Ability System (GAS) in UE5 |

**Realistic scope path:**
1. **Vertical slice (3–6 months, small team):** the Prologue only, with the nightclub raid, movement, bite mechanic, one Rook cinematic, and the Undercroft hub.
2. **Pitch it** with that slice to publishers or crowdfunding.
3. **Full game:** a large team and multi-year production.
